001 — La Filosofia

A cup of coffee,
the Italian way.

When Tony & Marina Horani opened United, they wanted Fort Wayne to feel what they grew up with — a café where the barista knows your name and your order, where coffee is poured slowly and savored. Not fuel. An invitation.

002 — What We Pour

Three things.
Done the old way.

01 — Caffè  ·  02 — Waffles  ·  03 — Welcome

/01

Caffè Italiano.

We pour Danesi Caffè — a Roman family roaster founded in 1905. Indiana's only café serving it. Pulled on a traditional machine, the way Marina's mother taught her in Italy.

  • Espresso · Macchiato · Cortado
  • Cappuccino · Flat White · Latte
  • Affogato · Iced Shakerato
  • Drip · Pour-over · Cold Brew
/02

Liège Waffles.

Made from Belgian pearl-sugar dough — caramelized crisp on the outside, pillow-soft inside. Topped with Nutella, fresh berries, mascarpone, lemon curd, or just a dusting of powdered sugar.

  • Classic — pearl sugar, dusted
  • Bacio — Nutella, hazelnut
  • Estate — strawberry, mascarpone
  • Limone — lemon curd, basil
/03

Benvenuti.

A small room, warm light, and someone who knows your name by your second visit. Bring your laptop. Bring your kids. Bring your future in-laws. We've got you a table.

  • Free Wi-Fi · plenty of outlets
  • Outdoor patio in season
  • Dog-friendly outside
  • Curbside & pickup orders
